# AI use

## Tools
- Claude (Anthropic), used in chat for guidance, commands and debugging.

## What I used it for
- Explaining the handout and planning the order of tasks.
- Writing and fixing shell commands, kubectl commands and small Python patches.
- Diagnosing problems: slow inference (PyTorch thread oversubscription under a 1-CPU limit), failed load tests, and missing cold-start log evidence.
- Drafting the NACL rules, subnet table and cost/SLO arithmetic.

## How I checked its output
- Ran every command myself and read the output before relying on it.
- Checked the NACL against `tools/nacl_sim.py` (6/6 correct).
- Checked model quality with `tools/score.py`, and timings with curl and Locust.
- Corrected AI mistakes when measurements disagreed with its suggestions (for example, the latency fix was confirmed by timing 0.55-1.1 s per request, down from 5-6 s).
- Teacher labels were generated by the Phi-3.5 model through Ollama and were not edited by hand.
